How to iterate: name a variant and what to change (pace, colour, text, camera, materials, whether the tagline appears). Elements can be mixed, for example V6's object with V12's lighting and V4's inscription. Everything is generated from one vector and 3D model of the mark, so a chosen design can be rendered at any resolution or length, with or without the fade-to-black for looping, or as a transparent PNG sequence for compositing.
